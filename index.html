Where do I start?

A quick-reference guide to what you need to read, who to train with, and what to get signed off before working on a platform, an activity, or something MRL hasn't done before. This is a starting checklist, not a substitute for the GSR's judgement on an unusual case.

Before anything else — mandatory for everyone

  1. Pass the online SGU course "Safety and security at ETH Zürich" (moodle-app2.let.ethz.ch)
  2. Register your phone number for emergency notifications
  3. Read the general MRL lab rules and floor emergency info — 02.01 Fire Safety (LEE J Floor), 12.3–12.5 extinguishers, defibrillator plan, assembly points

These are Part 1 of your Personal Safety Proof Card (Appendix 1.2). Sign off there before anything platform-specific below.

Pick what you'll actually be working with. The risk assessment (Appendix 9) is extra background, not required reading to get started.
